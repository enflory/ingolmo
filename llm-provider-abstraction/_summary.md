To enable seamless large language model (LLM) migrations across providers and applications, design your abstraction in two layers: an operational gateway (like LiteLLM Proxy, OpenRouter, Portkey, or a cloud-bundled equivalent) on the wire, paired with domain-specific, typed application interfaces using libraries such as Pydantic AI, Vercel AI SDK, Instructor, or BAML. Three critical rules ensure future-proofing: define ports in business terms rather than model semantics, treat model identity as runtime configuration rather than coding dependency, and manage prompts as immutable data outside application binaries. This architecture minimizes rewrites and technical debt as models and APIs evolve, and isolates cross-cutting concerns (billing, retries, guardrails, etc.) in the gateway. Rather than adopting a monolithic framework, splitting your abstraction along the wire-vs-semantics seam delivers resilience and reusability for multiple apps through model migrations.
