Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern proposes a schema-driven approach for using agentic LLMs to maintain personal, persistent, and compounding knowledge bases, shifting the "bookkeeping" burden from humans to AI. The system organizes information in a three-layer architecture and codifies operations—ingest, query, and lint—via a schema file (AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md), which governs directory structure, writing standards, taxonomy, and workflows. Within weeks of publication, a vibrant ecosystem of implementations and extensions emerged, spanning agent skill packs, full platforms, and critiques addressing scalability and data integrity challenges. While this pattern excels for curated, stable domains and small-team setups, it faces hurdles with knowledge base poisoning, scalability, and enterprise-grade concurrency. The central insight is that the schema—not the wiki content—serves as the transferable recipe enabling disciplined, evolving AI-powered research.
